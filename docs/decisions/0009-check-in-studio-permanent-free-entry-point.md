# 0009 Check-in Studio always has a free entry point

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Decided by: founder (Phil), 2026-10-30

## Context

The Phase 0 notes said a free tier is a required capability, and the earlier spec treated "free Check-in Studio as an acquisition route" as a hypothesis. This was conflict C-8.

## Decision

Check-in Studio must always have a free entry point. This is a permanent product principle, not a temporary trial or launch promotion.

## Not decided

- What the free entry point includes and where its limits are (for example capture types, guest numbers, branding).
- Whether any other product has a free entry point.
- Pricing, tiers, entitlements and paid features, which are deferred until after the MVP ([0010](0010-defer-monetisation-until-after-mvp.md)).

## Consequences

- Any future pricing model must keep a free Check-in Studio entry point.
- The decision constrains future monetisation but adds no billing work to the MVP.
- Free entry is a hypothesis about acquisition value, not a validated one. The free scope is decided with monetisation after the MVP.

## Evidence / sources

Phil's decision of 2026-10-30; master spec 4.1 and 9; conflict register C-8.
